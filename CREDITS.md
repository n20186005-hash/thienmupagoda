# Illustrations

The current guide uses an original SVG schematic, `public/images/tower-diagram.svg`, and its resized/padded social-card raster derivative. Created for this guide in 2026 and dedicated under CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . It is not a photograph, exact architectural survey or site plan. Visible notes identify this on both language pages and the social alt.

Previous remote Commons photographs by Dominik Tefert, Clay Gilliland and Arabsalam have been removed from delivered markup, CSS, social metadata and structured data. Their exact reuse and depicted-subject rights were not independently verified in this implementation; a previous generic credit is not treated as clearance.
